CZ

Chenyuan Zhang PhD

Research Fellow (Level B)

Monash University, Australia

I work on leveraging human modeling to enhance the performance and explainability of AI systems in the context of human–AI interaction. My research spans automated planning, computational cognitive modeling, and neuro-symbolic AI. Previously, I was a PhD student at the University of Melbourne, supervised by Associate Professor Nir Lipovetzky and Professor Charles Kemp.
